# The Adventures of Garfield*

_It should be noted that the subject is my wonderful cat and not Garfield_

## Description

For my photography assignment I decided that I wanted to take the opportunity to take my cat, Yoki, on an adventure. I think in the modern era we often times forget the perspective of our pets. Thus, for this assignment I wanted to take a step back and really give my cat the center of attention. I think this will create for an effective statement, as all good art should have, as well it will create some fun photos. I think it's such a great statement because of the recentering of our focus towards, not only someone else, but a pet that for many people becomes crucial to our lives that we never really understand quite as well.

## Narrative Approach

I'll be taking a linear approach

## Storyboard

I want my photos to represent the progression of my cat throughout an adventorous day as well as highlighting her perspective of the world.

1) A picture of my cat sleeping/ stretching out in the morning
2) Her look of concern as she gets into a car with a harness on
3) A picture "from her perspective" of whatever she ends up focusing on during the commute
4) A picture as she begins to explore
5) A couple pictures of the things she decides are worth exploring
6) The same as above
7) A picture of her plopping in some grass after she gets a little tired
8) A final picture of her curling up for the night after an exhausting day
