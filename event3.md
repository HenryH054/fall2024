# Event #3

For my final event I went to cornbelly's. It was meant to also be insanity point, but unfortunately insanity point was closed the night we went. Overall, I chose this event because I enjoy the spooky season and what better way to enjoy than to go to a spooky corn maze. Tragically though that didn't happen. As such we ended up spending a lot of time going through the park and seeing what activities were more YA friendly rather than family friendly. We ended up playing some minigolf, blowing a lot of bubbles, doing some water pump frog races, and going through some of their "haunted houses." It's a loose term since one was just the inside of an inflatable dragon. I think all in all I would still recommend this event even if we didn't get to do everything that I was hoping to do. That being said for future reference I would ensure that the corn maze is also open on the night that the group goes as to make it a little more worthwhile as in the end there was really only about an hour worths of events with just cornbellys for adults, with insanity point I think it would have been a solid two hours of fun though.
