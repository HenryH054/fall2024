# Soundings Journal

## Contextualize

I wanted to take this assignment quite seriously. As such I sat down with my girlfriend who spent all of her highschool years being a choir kid and I did something I hadn't ever done before, listen to an album starting at the beginning. To add to this, I, just a couple years ago, never listened to music. Even to this day I only listen to music occasionally and often times it is not the focus of my activity. This time was going to be different though. I say in my living room and connected it to one of my better speakers so that I could really focus on the music. My girlfriend then picked a pink floyd album that we could listen to.

## Takeaways

The particular album we listened to tends to be a stoner album and as such it has a few unique things to is such as pulsating loudness and interesting bridges. The most unique thing to me though is I finally understood why songs with long sweeping silence or songs without much music actually exist. In the particular album we listened to the first song was about two minutes the first minute was almost entirely silent and the second was a slow and steadly loudening heartbeat. This, a song I would always skip when playing on randomize, set the tone for the album. It then transitioned through three or four songs before I even noticed that a different song had started. Instead of these songs playing like an islands unto themselves engineered to maximize pleasure in a minimum time, as so much pop is, they each turned into a verse or bridge or chorus in this overarching story that drew me through and showed me the kind of artform music could be. A kind of art form that when one sits down to appreciate and focus on can truly tell a story of its own and lets the musician and listener feel a story.
