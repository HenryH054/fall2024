# Music in Advertising

Music is one of the most powerful tools we have available to us in modern day advertising. As I was briefly researching I found all sorts of interesting phenomena regarding music in advertising. For example, the EU has a mandate where music isn't allowed to endorse products, or even really mention them, without proper disclosures. It creates some interesting dynamics where artists will have to re-record and clip in over themselves for saying things even as basic as cherry cola. It was one of those things I never really questioned, why does the EU have this entirely overbearing rule, or on the flipside, why do I allow myself to listen to thinly vailed ads all day long. That's just the way things are.

As I was doing some cursory research in this I realized that the concept that things we allow to exist without question are often some of the most damning things came true yet again. In my research I realized that music is used in advertising because it more than any of our other senses instills in us feelings and desires. A picture looks cool and can be flashy, a scent may be nice(or headache inducing, looking at you Ulta,) a taste is nice(and unique,) feeling something can be a sensory dream, but music makes us feel. It makes us desire. It makes us want. It is deadly when we leave it unchecked and it's the reason the EU is so protective of music. A well made song can make consumers go crazy and not realize.

Needless to say I now realize how deadly music in advertising truly is. I also have come to the conclusion that I 'ought to make sure I know what I'm listening to. Maybe that music that subtly is pushing particular choices or particular products is having a more damaging effect on me than I realize. In addition it may be worth it to mute the ads once they roll. Blocking the sense that makes me desire and want certainly would be nice for my wallet.
